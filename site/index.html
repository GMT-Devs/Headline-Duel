FAQ

Questions, answered

Is it free?

Yes. There's no fee and no token. You only pay a little BOT Chain gas to start a duel or vote.

Can people vote twice?

No. The contract records one vote per wallet per duel, and creators can't vote on their own duels.

Why lock the AI pick?

So the AI can't peek at the votes. Its pick and a hash of its reason are stored on-chain when the duel starts — you can check the reason matches the hash.

Who can see results?

The creator always can. Voters see them right after voting, and everyone sees them once the duel ends. (Everything is on-chain, so the raw data is public — hiding results is a nudge against bandwagoning.)

Do I need a wallet to vote?

Yes, to keep it fair — one wallet, one vote. You can browse the wall and finished results without one.
